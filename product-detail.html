Product Description

DIN 931 hex head bolts are one of our most popular products. These bolts feature a hexagon-shaped head designed to be tightened with a wrench, providing high torque capability. The partial thread design allows for threading into a tapped hole or to be used with a nut.

Our manufacturing process ensures precise dimensional accuracy, consistent thread quality, and excellent mechanical properties. Each batch undergoes strict quality testing including tensile strength, hardness, and surface treatment inspection.

We maintain large inventory of commonly used sizes and can accommodate custom orders for special lengths, materials, or surface treatments. With our advanced production equipment and experienced engineering team, we guarantee consistent quality that meets or exceeds international standards.
