📦 Orders & Delivery

How long is your delivery time?

Generally it is 5-10 days if the goods are in stock. For out-of-stock items, delivery is typically 15-20 days depending on the quantity and production schedule. We maintain large inventory levels to ensure fast delivery for our most popular products.

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

Our standard MOQ varies by product type. For standard fasteners, MOQ typically ranges from 1,000 to 10,000 pieces depending on size and specification. For custom or non-standard parts, we can discuss flexible MOQs based on your requirements. We're happy to accommodate smaller trial orders for new customers.

Do you provide samples? Is it free or extra?

Yes, we offer free samples for most standard products from our existing inventory. Customers only need to cover the shipping cost. For custom samples requiring new tooling or special production, a sample fee may apply which can be credited against your first order.

💰 Payment & Pricing

What are your terms of payment?

Our standard payment terms are 30% T/T in advance with the balance due before shipment. For established customers, we offer flexible terms including L/C at sight and extended credit terms. Other payment methods like PayPal and Western Union are available for smaller orders.

What documents do you provide for customs clearance?

We provide a complete set of export documents including: Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Bill of Lading, Certificate of Origin (Form A, CO, etc.), and Material Test Certificates (MTC) upon request. We're experienced with various destination countries' requirements and can provide additional certifications as needed.

🌍 International Trade

Which countries do you export to?

We export to over 30 countries worldwide, including the United States, Germany, UK, Australia, Canada, Japan, South Korea, Brazil, and many others. Our experienced export team handles all shipping arrangements, documentation, and ensures compliance with each country's import regulations.

What international standards do your fasteners meet?

Our fasteners are manufactured to meet various international standards including DIN (German), ISO, ANSI/ASME (American), JIS (Japanese), BS (British), and other national standards. We can also produce parts according to your custom drawings or specifications. All products come with relevant certifications.

Do you offer OEM/ODM services for custom fasteners?

Yes, we specialize in OEM and ODM services. Our engineering team can work with your drawings, samples, or specifications to produce custom fasteners. We have experience with special materials, unique thread patterns, and application-specific designs. NDA agreements are available to protect your proprietary designs.
