SYM Fastener: Your Leading Partner for Global Riveting Solutions

Who we are established in 2015, Shanghai Yuanmao (SYM) has grown into a premier Chinese manufacturer and global supplier specializing in high-performance blind fasteners. With over a decade of industry expertise, we provide a comprehensive range of products, including Blind Rivets, Structural Rivets, Rivet Nuts, Self-clinching Fasteners (Studs, Standoffs, and Nuts), Cage Nuts, and professional Riveting Tools.

Advanced Manufacturing & Quality Control Our facility is equipped with a state-of-the-art set of production equipment and high-precision automatic lathes. By integrating international advanced standards into our daily operations, SYM ensures every component reaches a world-class level of durability and precision. We specialize in both standard fasteners (Auto standards/National standards) and customized non-standard parts tailored to specific engineering requirements.

Strategic Supply Chain & Logistics Headquartered in Shanghai, the heart of China’s industrial and logistics hub, SYM operates a sophisticated storage and logistics system. Our extensive sales network and complete supply chain allow us to offer the most competitive products with short lead times. We have strategically invested in leading industry enterprises to ensure we meet the diverse needs of customers in the automotive, solar, electrical, and construction sectors.

Our Purpose & Commitment At SYM, our purpose is simple: Quality and Professionalism. We are committed to meticulous customer service and transparent communication. On the basis of equality and mutual benefit, we aim to establish long-term, stable cooperative relations with partners worldwide for common development.

Experience the SYM difference—where precision manufacturing meets global service.
