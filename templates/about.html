Company Introduction

We are a specialist manufacturer and supplier in the fastener industry, focused on providing high-quality standard and custom fasteners to customers worldwide. With years of experience in manufacturing and exporting, we have built a strong reputation for quality, reliability, and excellent customer service.

Our company specializes in the supply of all standard DIN, BS, JIS and ISO fasteners and other industrial parts. Our customer base has grown to consist of hundreds of both domestic and foreign companies, including some renowned global corporations for which we have worked our way to participate in their various programs as an approved vendor.

Our main products include bolts, nuts, washers, thread rods, and non-standard custom parts to satisfy all customer requirements. As Quality is our lifeline and the main reason of our repeat business, we offer products of high quality, competitive prices, prompt deliveries and excellent after sales service.
