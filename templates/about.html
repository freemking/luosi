SYM Fastener: Your Leading Partner for Global Riveting Solutions

Established in 2015, Shanghai Yuanmao (SYM) has grown into a premier Chinese manufacturer and global supplier specializing in high-performance blind fasteners. With over a decade of industry expertise, we provide a comprehensive range of products, including Blind Rivets, Structural Rivets, Rivet Nuts, Self-clinching Fasteners (Studs, Standoffs, and Nuts), Cage Nuts, and professional Riveting Tools.

Our Story

Advanced Manufacturing & Quality Control

Our facility is equipped with a state-of-the-art set of production equipment and high-precision automatic lathes. By integrating international advanced standards into our daily operations, SYM ensures every component reaches a world-class level of durability and precision.

We specialize in both standard fasteners (Auto standards/National standards) and customized non-standard parts tailored to specific engineering requirements.

Strategic Supply Chain & Logistics

Headquartered in Shanghai, the heart of China's industrial and logistics hub, SYM operates a sophisticated storage and logistics system. Our extensive sales network and complete supply chain allow us to offer the most competitive products with short lead times.

We have strategically invested in leading industry enterprises to ensure we meet the diverse needs of customers in the automotive, solar, electrical, and construction sectors.
